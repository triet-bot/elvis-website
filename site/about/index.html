About Top Creative Nails

Top Creative Nails is a neighborhood nail salon at 830 Mayfield Rd, Suite 500, Grand Prairie, TX 75052. Inside you will find a row of white massage spa chairs for pedicures, manicure stations along the wall and a full wall of polish colors.

Creative is in the name for a reason. Our techs build long acrylic sets, strong dip powder, crisp French tips and custom nail art from whatever photo you bring. We also fix sets that went wrong elsewhere.

What clients mention most is how they feel here: a clean salon, friendly staff who take their time, and nails that come out exactly the way they asked.
