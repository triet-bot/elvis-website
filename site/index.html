Good to know

Questions, answered

Where is Top Creative Nails?

We are at 830 Mayfield Rd, Suite 500, Grand Prairie, TX 75052. Get directions and hours.

What are your hours?

Monday to Thursday 10 AM to 7 PM, Friday and Saturday 9 AM to 7 PM. We are closed on Sunday.

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes. Walk-ins are welcome during open hours. The salon can get busy, especially on weekends, so call ahead at (972) 264-2744 if you want a set time.

How do I book an appointment?

Call us at (972) 264-2744 or send a message on Facebook. See all the ways to book.

What services do you offer?

Acrylic nails (full sets and fills), dip powder, gel and classic manicures, pedicures, French tips, nail art, nail repair, eyebrow waxing and kids polish.
