Dip vs acrylic vs gel: which nails are right for you?

Dip powder, acrylic and gel are the three most popular ways to get nails that last longer than regular polish. They look similar when they are done, but they wear differently. Here is how they compare, so you know what to ask for at Top Creative Nails in Grand Prairie.

Gel: glossy color on your natural nails

A gel manicure is polish that cures under an LED lamp, so it is completely dry before you leave and shines like glass. It is thin and flexible, which makes it feel the most like your own nails. Choose gel when you want color that outlasts regular polish but you do not need extra length.

Dip powder: strength without the bulk

Dip powder builds color in thin layers of powder that harden into a tough, chip-resistant finish. It is a favorite for people who work with their hands. Dip can be done on natural nails, or with tips for added length. One of our clients told us her powder nails were the strongest she had ever worn.

Acrylic: length, shape and room for art

Acrylic is the go-to for long coffins, almonds and stilettos. It is the strongest option for length and gives the most room for nail art, chrome and rhinestones. Acrylic sets need a fill every two to three weeks as your nails grow out.

Still not sure?

Tell your tech how long you want your nails, how hard you are on your hands and how often you want to come back. They will recommend the best fit. Want a French tip? It works on all three. Browse our gallery for ideas, then call or walk in.

Common questions

Which lasts longest: dip, acrylic or gel?

Dip powder usually wears the longest between visits, often three to four weeks. Acrylic lasts as long as you keep up with fills every two to three weeks. Gel typically lasts about two to three weeks.

Which is best for long nails?

Acrylic is the best choice for long, dramatic shapes like coffin and stiletto. See our acrylic nails page.

Can I switch from acrylic to dip or gel?

Yes. Your tech can remove your current set and start fresh with a different option. Mention it when you call so we can plan the time.
