Frequently asked questions

Where is Top Creative Nails?

We are at 830 Mayfield Rd, Suite 500, Grand Prairie, TX 75052. Get directions and hours.

What are your hours?

Monday to Thursday 10 AM to 7 PM, Friday and Saturday 9 AM to 7 PM. We are closed on Sunday.

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes. Walk-ins are welcome during open hours. The salon can get busy, especially on weekends, so call ahead at (972) 264-2744 if you want a set time.

How do I book an appointment?

Call us at (972) 264-2744 or send a message on Facebook. See all the ways to book.

What services do you offer?

Acrylic nails (full sets and fills), dip powder, gel and classic manicures, pedicures, French tips, nail art, nail repair, eyebrow waxing and kids polish.

Can you fix nails that were done at another salon?

Yes. Clients regularly come to us after a set or design went wrong somewhere else, and we rebuild it the way they wanted. See nail repair and redo.

How much does a full set cost?

Prices depend on the service, length, shape and how much design you add. Call (972) 264-2744 or ask at the front desk for today's price before we start.

Can I bring my kids?

Yes. Kids can get a polish change or a mini manicure while you get your nails done. See kids manicure and polish.

Do you do eyebrow waxing?

Yes. Eyebrow waxing is a quick add-on to any manicure or pedicure.

Can I bring a photo of the design I want?

Please do. Show your tech a photo or screenshot and we will match it. Browse our nail gallery for ideas, or follow us on Instagram at @thetopcreativenails.
