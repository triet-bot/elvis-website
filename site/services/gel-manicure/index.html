Gel manicure in Grand Prairie, TX

Pricing depends on length, shape and design. Call or ask at the front desk for today's price.

A gel manicure starts like a classic manicure, then swaps regular polish for gel color cured under an LED lamp. You leave with nails that are fully dry and shine like glass.

Gel is a good middle ground when you want color that lasts but do not want added length. If you want length or strength, ask about acrylic or dip powder instead.

Aftercare tips

  • Gel is cured and dry before you leave, so you can use your hands right away.
  • Do not peel or pick off gel. It can take layers of your natural nail with it.
  • Come back for professional removal when you are ready for a new color.

Gel manicure questions

How long does a gel manicure last?

Most clients get about two to three weeks before they want a change, depending on how fast their nails grow and how hard they are on their hands.

Is gel the same as dip or acrylic?

No. Gel is a polish cured under a lamp on your natural nails. Dip powder and acrylic build a stronger layer and can add length. See our dip vs acrylic vs gel guide.

Can you take off my old gel before the new one?

Yes. Mention it when you check in so we can include removal in your appointment.
