Aftercare tips

  • Give polish a few minutes to set before snacks or play.
  • Kids' nails grow fast, so a quick polish change every week or two keeps them neat.
  • Bring them along on your next visit for a matching color.

Kids manicure and polish questions

What age can kids get their nails done?

Kids visiting with a parent can get a polish change or a gentle mini manicure. Ask at the front desk if you are not sure what fits your child.

Can my child sit with me during my appointment?

Yes. Many parents book their own pedicure and have their child's nails done during the same visit.

What polish do you use for kids?

Kids get regular polish for a simple, gentle polish change.
