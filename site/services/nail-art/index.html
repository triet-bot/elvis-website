Aftercare tips

  • Rhinestones and 3D accents can catch on fabric for the first day, so be gentle.
  • A clear top coat refresh helps art stay glossy.
  • Save a photo of your set if you want the same design again next time.

Nail art and designs questions

Can you copy a design from a photo?

Yes. Show your tech a screenshot or Pinterest photo and they will match it as closely as possible.

Can I get nail art on short natural nails?

Yes. Simple designs, lines and accents work on short nails too, like the zebra set in our gallery.

Do you do designs on toes?

Yes. Hand-painted designs and gems can be added during a pedicure.
