How long it takes

How long it lasts
Usually 3 to 5 weeks before regrowth needs a cleanup

Aftercare tips

  • Skip makeup and heavy creams on the area for the rest of the day.
  • Avoid hot showers, saunas and direct sun on fresh skin for about a day.
  • Let brows grow for a few weeks between waxes for the best shape.

Eyebrow waxing questions

Can I add eyebrow waxing to my nail appointment?

Yes. It is a quick add-on to any manicure or pedicure. Just ask at check-in.

Will you keep my natural brow shape?

Yes. The goal is to clean up stray hairs and define your natural arch. Tell your tech how full you like your brows.

How often should I wax my eyebrows?

Most people come back every three to five weeks.
