The French tip never goes out of style, and we do every version of it: the soft, short square French shown here, a deep French that runs halfway down the nail, colored or chrome tips, and Frenches finished with a single crystal accent.

French tips work on acrylic, gel and dip powder, so pick the base that fits how long you want them to last.

Time and wear

How long it takes
Depends on the base: about 45 minutes on gel or dip, longer on a new acrylic set
How long it lasts
Same as the base you choose: about 2 to 3 weeks for gel, 3 to 4 for dip, and fills every 2 to 3 weeks for acrylic

Aftercare tips

  • White tips show wear first, so avoid using your nails as tools.
  • Use cuticle oil to keep the nude base looking clean as nails grow.
  • Book a fill or refresh when the grow-out gap starts to show.

French tip nails questions

What is a deep French?

A deep French runs the white tip much farther down the nail for a bold, modern look. It works best on medium to long nails.

Can I get colored French tips?

Yes. Any shade, chrome or glitter can be used for the tip.

Can I get a French tip on my natural nails?

Yes, with a gel manicure or dip powder. For extra length, choose acrylic.
