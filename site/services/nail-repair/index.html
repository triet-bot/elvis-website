Aftercare tips

  • Do not use nail glue at home on a lifted nail. It can trap moisture underneath.
  • Come in as soon as a nail lifts or cracks so it can be fixed before it breaks.
  • Keep up with fills to prevent future breaks.

Nail repair and redo questions

Can you fix nails that were done somewhere else?

Yes. We regularly fix and redo sets from other salons, including dip and acrylic sets that came out lumpy or uneven.

Do I need a whole new set if one nail breaks?

Usually not. One broken or lifted nail can be repaired on its own.

What if I do not like my nail art from another salon?

Bring it in. We can rework the design or start fresh with new nail art.
