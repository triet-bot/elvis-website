Pedicure in Grand Prairie, TX

Pricing depends on length, shape and design. Call or ask at the front desk for today's price.

Our pedicures happen in a row of white massage spa chairs along the salon wall. You soak, your tech trims and shapes your toenails, tidies the cuticles, smooths rough heels and finishes with a lower-leg massage and polish.

Pedicures are what many of our regulars come back for. One client wrote that she always gets her pedicures here and called us "the best in GP". Want designs on your toes? Our nail art works on toes too, from hand-painted designs to gems. Or pair it with a manicure.

Time and wear

How long it takes
About 45 minutes to 1 hour
How long it lasts
Polish usually lasts 2 to 3 weeks on toes, longer with gel

Aftercare tips

  • Wear open-toe shoes home so fresh polish does not smudge.
  • Moisturize your heels daily to keep calluses from building back up.
  • Book every 3 to 4 weeks to keep feet smooth and polish fresh.

Pedicure questions

What is included in a pedicure?

A warm soak in a massage spa chair, toenail trimming and shaping, cuticle care, callus smoothing, a lower-leg massage and polish of your choice.

Can I get gel polish on my toes?

Yes. Gel on toes is dry before you leave and lasts longer than regular polish.

Can I get a manicure and pedicure at the same time?

Yes. Ask for both when you call or check in. Many clients pair a pedicure with a classic or gel manicure.
