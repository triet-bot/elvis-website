Dip powder nails in Grand Prairie, TX

Pricing depends on length, shape and design. Call or ask at the front desk for today's price.

Dip powder builds color and strength in thin layers of powder, sealed for a smooth, glossy finish. It is lighter than acrylic and tougher than regular polish, which makes it a favorite for people who are hard on their hands.

One client who came in for powder nails told us they were the strongest nails she had ever worn. Another came in after a dip job at a different salon went wrong, and we gave her a new set with the design she wanted. See nail repair.
