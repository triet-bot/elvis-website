5. Giveaway: "Holiday Glow-Up Giveaway" (Nov 9–22, 2026)

Rule #1: never tie the giveaway to reviews. No "review to enter" and no bonus entries for reviews. Google removes incentivized reviews and can flag the listing, and the FTC's 2024 rule bans rewarding reviews. Review asks stay separate, with nothing attached.
ItemPlan
Prizes1 grand prize: full set plus pedicure. 2 runner-ups: gel manicure each. Redeem by end of January. Keep each prize under $600.
